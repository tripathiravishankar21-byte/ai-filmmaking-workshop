About the Workshop

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping how stories are imagined, produced and shared. From scripting and concept visualisation to editing and post-production, intelligent tools are expanding what creators can do and how quickly ideas can become images on screen. This workshop, organised by COMEST, brings together computer science and cinema to look at how AI is influencing filmmaking, visual storytelling and modern media, with Dr. Vishnu Shankar of the Department of Computer Science as resource person.
